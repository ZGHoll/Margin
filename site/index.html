About Crafter's Ledger

What this is

Crafter's Ledger ranks crafting recipes in WoW: Forever by how much gold each craft is expected to earn. It reads auction house prices every hour, works out what a craft costs to make and what the result sells for, and lists the best ones first.

Choose a profession and your auction house at the top of the page. Everything else updates on its own.

It's a free community tool, built and kept running by Suln.

How profit is worked out

profit = items made x sell price x (1 - auction house cut) - reagent cost
  • Reagent prices come from the auction house. If an item is not listed there, its vendor price is used.
  • Sell and buy prices use the 25th percentile by default: the price at which the cheapest quarter of items for sale are available. It is steadier than the single lowest listing, which can be a one-off undercut. You can switch to Lowest or Median.
  • Return is profit divided by reagent cost, so it shows how much you get back per gold spent.

Using it well

  • Pick the right auction house. Prices differ between houses.
  • Set a minimum number of listings to avoid items that rarely sell.
  • Click any recipe to see each reagent and whether its price is from the auction house, a vendor or a hand-set value.
  • Untick "Hide recipes missing a price" to see recipes the data could not fully price.

Good to know

  • Prices are hourly snapshots and can lag what you see in game.
  • Deposits are not included. The cut is 5% at faction houses and 15% at neutral ones; pick yours in the filters.
  • Blizzard has not published official documentation for WoW: Forever's data, so some items or recipes may be missing or change without notice.
  • Treat every number as an estimate and check the in-game auction house before you commit a lot of gold.
